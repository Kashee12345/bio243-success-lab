# BIO 243 Success Lab

An optional companion website for **BIOL U243 Human Anatomy and Physiology I at USC Upstate**. It covers time management, study skills and using AI the right way, all practiced on real BIO 243 content.

**Live site:** https://kashee12345.github.io/bio243-success-lab/

## What is inside
- **15 weekly steps** (generic semester, no dates). Each week has one big idea, a five-minute action, one tool, quick checks and a private reflection.
- **Tools:** Time Budget Planner, Exam Planner (with final-exam mode and calendar export), Spaced Review Tracker, Study Habits Self-Check and Exam Wrapper.
- **The BIO 243 Success Book:** 14 chapters, about 55,000 words, with diagrams, quick checks and sources. It can also be printed as one PDF.
- **Flashcards:** 170 cards from the book's key terms plus the five AI rules.
- **Games:** The Success Chase, AI: Smart or Shady?, and the AI Prompt Lab.
- **Printables:** semester planner, exam prep checklist, reflection journal and My Study System.

## Privacy
There are no accounts, analytics or tracking. Anything a student types is saved only in their own browser (localStorage).
