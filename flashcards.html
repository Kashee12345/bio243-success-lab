    Make your own A&P cards the right way

    • One idea per card. "Osteoclast: breaks down bone matrix" beats a paragraph.
    • Quiz both directions: term to meaning, and meaning to term.
    • Make contrast cards for look-alikes (osteoblast vs osteoclast, afferent vs efferent).
    • Say the answer before you flip. Recognizing is not the same as remembering.
    • Keep the cards you miss in play until you get them. CircleIn flashcards count toward your BIO 243 grade, so build them there too.
