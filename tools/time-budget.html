    Rules of thumb
    • The BIO 243 syllabus suggests about 4 to 6 hours a week for lecture plus 4 to 6 for lab.
    • Use blocks of 25 to 50 minutes, spread across at least four days.
    • Protect 7 to 9 hours of sleep. A tired brain learns less per hour.
    • Add one backup block for the week that goes sideways.
