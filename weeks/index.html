The 15 weeks

One short page a week: one big idea, a five-minute action, and one tool, all practiced on real BIO 243 content. Start with whatever week of the semester you are in. Your checkmarks and notes stay on this device.

Weeks 1 to 4

Start strong

The first four weeks decide a lot. Build the habits before Exam 1, not after it.

  • DoneWeek 1Survival KitWhy A&P feels different, and how to set yourself up to win.
  • DoneWeek 2Your Real WeekFind the hours. Protect them. Build a plan that survives real life.
  • DoneWeek 3Read Like a ScientistThe textbook is a tool. Learn to use it on purpose.
  • DoneWeek 4Memory That LastsRereading feels good. Testing yourself works. Start your exam plan.
Weeks 5 to 8

Build your system

Exam 1 and Exam 2 live here. Plan backward, read your results, and get better each time.

  • DoneWeek 5Exam WeekWalk in prepared, calm, and with a plan for every question.
  • DoneWeek 6Read Your ResultsYour exam is data. Use it to change what you do next.
  • DoneWeek 7Structures and ProcessesHow to learn the body's parts and the body's steps.
  • DoneWeek 8Plan Backward, AgainExam 2 is coming. Use what Exam 1 taught you.
Weeks 9 to 12

Stay steady

The middle of the term is where people drift. Check in, use AI the right way, and protect your energy.

  • DoneWeek 9Midterm Check-InAn honest look at where you are, and a plan to finish.
  • DoneWeek 10AI as a TutorMake AI quiz you, explain things another way, and check your thinking.
  • DoneWeek 11Smart or Shady?Know where the line is, in BIO 243 and in every other class.
  • DoneWeek 12Stress, Sleep and SetbacksProtect the brain that does all your studying.
Weeks 13 to 15

Finish strong

Exam 3, the cumulative final, and the skills you carry into BIO 244 and beyond.

  • DoneWeek 13Exam 3 WeekBring the whole system: plan, mix, sleep, strategy.
  • DoneWeek 14Final CountdownA cumulative final needs a cumulative plan. Weakest topics first.
  • DoneWeek 15Finish Strong, Look AheadTake the final, then take your system with you.
