Survival Kit

Why A&P feels different, and how to set yourself up to win.

In BIO 243 around now

BIO 243 usually opens with Chapter 1 (the language of anatomy, body regions, homeostasis) and Chapter 4 (tissues). That is a lot of new words in the very first week, and that is normal.

The big idea

A&P is not hard because you are not smart enough. It is hard because it asks you to learn a huge new vocabulary and then use it to explain how the body works, every week, without a break. Many strong high school students hit a wall in the first month because the study habits that worked before were built for lighter courses.

The good news: the skills that work in A&P are learnable, and they are the same skills nursing and health-science programs expect. This Success Lab walks you through them one week at a time. Each week is short: one idea, one five-minute action, and one tool.

This week has one job: know how the course works and where help lives, before you need it.

A&P in action

Directional terms are your first test of this system. Superior, inferior, anterior, posterior, medial, lateral, proximal and distal come up in every chapter after this one. Learn them now, with your body: point to your own wrist and say "distal to the elbow." Ten minutes of that this week saves you hours later.

Smart AI move

It is fine to ask AI to quiz you on directional terms. It is not fine to use AI on a quiz. Read the five AI rules on the home page this week.

Check yourself

Where should you look first for how BIO 243 is graded and when work is due?

  1. A classmate's notes
  2. The syllabus and Blackboard
  3. An AI chatbot
  4. Last semester's group chat

The syllabus and Blackboard are the official sources. Classmates and AI can be wrong or out of date.

You feel lost in week 1 because there are so many new terms. What does that most likely mean?

  1. You do not belong in A&P
  2. You should drop the course now
  3. You are having a normal first-week reaction to a vocabulary-heavy course
  4. You need to study only the night before exams

Almost everyone feels this at first. Struggle early is a signal to build a system, not a sign you do not belong.

Reflect

What worried you most about BIO 243 before the first class? What is one thing you will do differently than you did in high school?
