BIO 243 exam prep checklist

Exam: ____________   Date: ____________   Chapters: ______________________

7 to 10 days before
  • Find the exam date and chapters in the syllabus.
  • Build a plan in the Exam Planner and add it to your calendar.
  • List your shaky topics in the Spaced Review Tracker.
  • Check which Reading Guide sections count for this exam.
3 to 6 days before
  • Blank-page recall for each chapter, then check with OpenStax.
  • Practice questions without notes; review every miss.
  • Mixed sets: shuffle questions from different chapters.
  • Bring one specific question to student hours or an SI session.
1 to 2 days before
  • One timed, mixed practice set across every chapter.
  • Finish the Reading Guide (usually due the night before at 11:59 p.m.).
  • Make a one-page brain dump of the hardest facts.
The night before
  • Light review of your brain dump only. No new material.
  • Pack what you need (ID, pencils, water, a charged device if online).
  • Set an alarm and get 7 to 9 hours of sleep.
Exam day
  • Eat something and arrive early.
  • Three slow breaths before you start.
  • Read every stem; watch NOT and EXCEPT; predict before looking.
  • Skip and return when you stall. Change an answer only with a clear reason.
After the exam
  • Fill in the Exam Wrapper within a few days.
  • Add missed topics to the Spaced Review Tracker (they may show up on the final).
  • Write one change for the next exam.
